02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Creative mind.
Curious by nature.

H✳

I'm Hyunwoo Song, a design graduate from the University of California, Davis.

My interests span graphic design, UI/UX, digital experiences, and game interfaces. I enjoy bringing together visuals and functionality — from shaping a clear layout to building an interactive prototype.

I work in English and Korean, and I value thoughtful details, accessible experiences, and learning through making.
