01

Two players take turns rolling two dice. The first to reach 30 points wins.

02

If neither die shows 1, add both dice to your score. Roll again or pass.

03

Roll one 1 and your turn ends. Roll two 1s and your total score resets to zero.

04

Pass to hand the dice to the other player without risking a penalty.
